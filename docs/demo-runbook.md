# Three-minute pilot runbook

Use the local PostgreSQL app. Select **Demo AI** before the pitch; it is deterministic, requires no key and avoids the first-time Local CLIP model download. Local CLIP and Gemini remain optional review paths outside the timed demo.

Before each rehearsal, press **Reset demo** and wait for saving to finish. This creates a separate simulated workspace without deleting any ledger history. Keep Demo Operator selected.

1. **0:00–0:30 — Brand inventory.** Open Brand Studio, change Product to **Denim Jeans - simulated pilot**, and press **Save new batch & analyse**. Keep all numeric inputs at their defaults. This creates fresh, unapproved inventory for the explicit approval demonstration; B017 already has approved concepts and an allocated Drop. Show the circular route hierarchy. Keep, Repair and Resell remain ahead of Remix; recycling is fallback.
2. **0:30–0:55 — Proposal and approval.** Generate Demo AI proposals. Select Denim Tote, confirm deterministic feasibility, verify the simulated inventory inspection, then press **Register recipe & approve pilot**. Explain that images are illustrative and the maker review is simulated.
3. **0:55–1:20 — Market test.** Launch the Drop and show confirmed demand, planned BOM material and the 68-unit waste-defined maximum.
4. **1:20–2:15 — Genuine shortage.** Use **New genuine-shortage scenario**. It starts with 41 confirmed buyers, 41.2 kg allocated and no authorized compatible stock beyond the plan. Open Scan an item and explicitly select **Demo AI · mock fallback** after the scanner loads (a configured Gemini provider is otherwise the default). Enter **Denim jeans, cotton denim, damaged with reusable panels, no longer used.** and press **Scan clothing**. Review the Unknown material suggestion, explicitly select Cotton Denim for this known simulated fixture, apply reviewed suggestions and confirm the item, reserve the hypothetical collection point, simulate receipt and inspection, then allocate the verified 0.8 kg. The bounty bonus appears only because authorized stock cannot cover the planned 42-unit BOM.
5. **2:15–2:40 — Checkout and unlock.** Return to the homepage, open **Denim Utility Bag**, then **Preorder**. Select the 10% return entitlement and redeem all 194 verified credits. Reserve benefits, confirm the simulated preorder, choose Continue shopping, then open Scan an item to show the automatic unlock. The individual simulated payable is SGD 42.16 (49 - 4.90 - 1.94). The 42nd confirmed order joins the now-ready 42 kg BOM and unlocks production. Show `42 × SGD 49 = SGD 2,058` committed GMV and keep net simulated sales separate if incentives apply.
6. **2:40–3:00 — Economics.** Open Brand Studio and select **DROP024 · Denim Utility Bag** in Financial outlook Drop. Show 42 confirmed orders and capacity 42. For a conservative rounded incentive forecast, enter SGD 0.12 return discount and SGD 0.05 redeemed credits per unit. These rounded scenario assumptions produce SGD 2,050.86 forecast net sales; actual stored Drop commitments are SGD 2,051.16 after the one discounted checkout. The admin summary is workspace-wide, including the separate seeded Tote, and must not be described as this Drop’s revenue. State that every cost is an editable assumption and the result is forecast contribution, not realized profit.

All consumers, preorders, returns, locations, inspections and maker approvals in this flow are clearly labelled simulated or hypothetical. Do not present the seed as customers, partnerships or measured environmental impact.

## Regenerating seeded concepts

Generate Remix Opportunities rechecks current available material. Existing brand/maker-approved records are reused without changing their frozen economics or approval timestamps. An allocated Tote continues to its existing Drop; alternatives without available stock remain unavailable for launch. Regeneration does not approve a recipe or create a second Drop. A new proposal demonstration uses the fresh batch above. Each genuine-shortage scenario creates another isolated, labelled simulated workspace; stock is never silently shared between scenarios.

Wait for each stage heading and saving indicator to settle before the next click. The timed browser walkthroughs include UI/server waiting and tool overhead, but are not a recorded spoken presentation. Leave room for narration.
