# Three-minute pilot runbook

Use the local PostgreSQL app. Select **Demo AI** before the pitch; it is deterministic, requires no key and avoids the first-time Local CLIP model download. Local CLIP and Gemini remain optional review paths outside the timed demo.

1. **0:00–0:30 — Brand inventory.** Open Brand Studio, use the seeded denim batch and show the circular route hierarchy. Keep, Repair and Resell remain ahead of Remix; recycling is fallback.
2. **0:30–0:55 — Proposal and approval.** Generate Demo AI proposals. Select Denim Tote, confirm deterministic feasibility, verify the simulated inventory inspection, then press **Register recipe & approve pilot**. Explain that images are illustrative and the maker review is simulated.
3. **0:55–1:20 — Market test.** Launch the Drop and show confirmed demand, planned BOM material and the 68-unit waste-defined maximum.
4. **1:20–2:15 — Genuine shortage.** Use **New genuine-shortage scenario**. It starts with 41 confirmed buyers, 41.2 kg allocated and no authorized compatible stock beyond the plan. Scan the default jeans with Demo AI, review the fields, confirm the item, reserve the hypothetical collection point, simulate receipt and inspection, then allocate the verified 0.8 kg. The bounty bonus appears only because authorized stock cannot cover the planned 42-unit BOM.
5. **2:15–2:40 — Checkout and unlock.** Complete the simulated checkout. The 42nd confirmed order joins the now-ready 42 kg BOM and unlocks production. Show `42 × SGD 49 = SGD 2,058` committed GMV and keep net simulated sales separate if incentives apply.
6. **2:40–3:00 — Economics.** Open Brand Studio margin comparison. State that every cost is an editable assumption and the result is forecast contribution, not realized profit.

All consumers, preorders, returns, locations, inspections and maker approvals in this flow are clearly labelled simulated or hypothetical. Do not present the seed as customers, partnerships or measured environmental impact.
